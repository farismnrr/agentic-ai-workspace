# Inbox and tasks

## Review work in your Inbox

The Inbox shows tasks waiting for your answer, review or attention. Only tasks
ready for review can be marked done from the Inbox.

- Select review items with their checkboxes, then choose **Mark selected done**.
- **Select all eligible** selects every visible review item.
- **Mark all eligible done** completes every visible review item, including
  items you have not selected. The button shows how many tasks it will affect.
- Questions and failed tasks stay in the Inbox until you answer or continue
  them. Their checkboxes are unavailable for marking done.
- If some tasks cannot be completed, the Inbox reports the partial result and
  keeps failed items selected so you can try again.

## Find tasks in Settings

Open **Settings → Tasks** to see tasks across your projects. Filter by status or
project, sort by recent update, title or status, then select a task to open its
details. Change filters when no tasks match them.
