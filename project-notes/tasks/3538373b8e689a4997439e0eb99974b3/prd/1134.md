## Findings

### What exists
- **Migration `061_work_reported.sql`**: adds nullable `reported_at`. It is stored and read in `sqlite/work_store.go:15,59` and `work_queries.go:85`.
- **Domain**: `ReportedAt` is on `ExternalWork` and `WorkSummary`.
- **`DeriveWorkPhase`** (`domain/work_phase.go`): terminal > awaiting person > blocked > reported > idle > running. It drives `Stale` in `window.go` and `work_state_support.go`.
- **Config** (`platform/config/work_settings.go`): reads all four `WORK_*` settings into `Config`. The sweep mode defaults to `dry-run`.
- **Store clock**: `NewStoreWithClock` is in use.
- **`UpdateWorkCall`**: bumps the work's `updated_at`, except for `interrupted` calls.
- **`Works` and `Service`**: `Works` takes `WorkPhaseThresholds`, and `Service.UseWorkIdleAfter` is wired in `bootstrap/agent.go`.
- **Test schema**: `test/go/modules/tasksqlite/work_test_schema_test.go` already has `reported_at`.
- **`Service.Accept`**: turns a `review` task into `done`. The "Ready for your review" label exists in `web/.../content/status.ts`.

### What is missing
1. **Nothing writes or clears `reported_at`.** `WorkPatch`, `advanceWork`, `touchWorkCall` and `UpdateWorkCall` never touch it, so the "reported" phase can never happen today.
2. **Config clamp blocks fast tests.** `WORK_ABANDON_AFTER` is forced to at least `WORK_IDLE_AFTER`+30m, so a scenario cannot use second-scale windows. The clamp must become "greater than idle". The 1h–3h warning stays.
3. **No reconciler.** `WORK_SWEEP_MODE`, `WORK_SWEEP_EVERY` and `thresholds.AbandonAfter` are read but never used. Bootstrap only starts `handoffs.Run`.
4. **Hard-coded abandon rules.** `abandonStale` uses 24 hours and its event text says "24 hours". `abandonIdleAtCap` uses a fixed 30 minutes instead of the idle setting.
5. **`complete_work` repeat.** A repeat succeeds only when the summary matches (`completionMatches`). The `ErrWorkClosed` and `ErrAwaitingPerson` texts are unchanged.
6. **MCP instructions.** `server.go:104–114` still never mention `complete_work`.
7. **No Confirm.** There is no endpoint, `WorkSummary` has no `phase`, and the SSE heartbeat (`stream.go` `push(force=false)`) stays silent when time alone changes the phase.
8. **Web.** `working-indicator.ts` and `TaskHeader.svelte` are unchanged. A stale task still offers Stop but no Mark complete. `logic/work-status.ts` already exists (48 lines), so extend it instead of creating `work-phase.ts`.
9. **Compose files.** `compose.dev.yaml` passes no `WORK_*` variables to the container. The person cannot flip `WORK_SWEEP_MODE` through `.env` until they are listed there (check `compose.prod.yaml` and `compose.latest.yaml` too).
10. **Tests.** There is no `07-external-work-lifecycle.test.sh` and no lifecycle browser flow.

## Rollout and rollback
- **Rollout:** ship with `WORK_SWEEP_MODE` at its default `dry-run`. The person reads the "would abandon" log lines for a day or two. If they look right, the person sets `on` and redeploys.
- **Rollback:** set `WORK_SWEEP_MODE=off` and redeploy.
- **Migration:** 061 only adds a nullable column and stays.
- **Already-ended work:** it stays ended. The person can Accept a `review` task or Continue a failed one.

## What changed vs the old PRD
- Slice 1 is mostly shipped (061, `DeriveWorkPhase`, config, store clock, `UpdateWorkCall` touch). The old step 1 shrinks to the config clamp fix, because nothing writes `reported_at` yet.
- The window is 1–3h, with a 2h default, instead of 24h plus a 30m cap rule. The 24h `abandonStale` is removed.
- Rollout is log-only first.
- Reported then cleaned up ends in `review` ("Ready for your review"). Unreported ends failed "Lost contact". Inactivity never becomes done or proven.
- The old "extend `work-phase.ts`" becomes "extend `work-status.ts`", per the reuse rule.
- Tests are Bash plus Playwright only, all under `test/`. Fast CI stays guardrails plus `make backend-test`. Full carries the black-box and e2e runs.
- New items: the compose pass-through for `WORK_*`, and a `lifecycle` branch in `test/e2e/run.sh`.
